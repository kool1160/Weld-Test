# Research

Cross-project research and reusable lessons from this project are cataloged in the central private research library:

**`DumpsterFireWorks/Projects-research--library-`**

The library is the discovery index for reusable welding, fabrication, training/test design, experiment, audit, and evidence-backed findings across projects.

This repository remains authoritative for current project content/status and original artifacts. Original source paths and commits remain authoritative provenance. Cataloging research in the library does not change project authority.

Before archiving or deleting this repository or any research-bearing branch, issue, PR, experiment, or document, verify that reusable research has been cataloged in the library. Do not move employer/customer data, proprietary files, credentials, or sensitive evidence into it.
